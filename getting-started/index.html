A reviewed introduction

Artan Records welcomes artist introductions and creative proposals. The current route is a human-reviewed enquiry, not instant distribution or self-service registration.

What to prepare

Your artist name, a short introduction and a listening link are enough to begin. Include the release type, contributors and intended timing if known.

What follows

If there is a fit, the next step is a conversation about scope and terms. Submission alone does not create representation or an artist account.
