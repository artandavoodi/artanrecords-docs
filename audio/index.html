Prepare your audio

Preserve the master and share safely.

For initial reviewFor an accepted releaseBefore delivery

For initial review

A listening link is sufficient. Do not place private master files or production sessions in a public repository.

For an accepted release

Keep the original lossless master and a clear version history. Confirm the final track order and durations.

Before delivery

Confirm sample rate, bit depth, channel format and delivery method with the label. No upload portal or automated audio quality-control service is currently available.
