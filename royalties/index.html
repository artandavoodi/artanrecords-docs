Royalties and statements

Understand the terms before interpreting a balance.

Your agreement governs

There is no universal royalty percentage, deduction policy or payout schedule published here. Those must be defined in an approved agreement.

Listening data is not money

Streams and audience analytics are different from reported royalties, reconciled receipts and payable amounts. Studio currently displays none of these as real account data.

Ask about a statement

If you receive a statement, retain its period and reference when raising a question. A secure statement portal is not yet available.
