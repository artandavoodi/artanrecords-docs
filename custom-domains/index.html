Website and store domains

Manage separate custom-domain requests for your artist website and store in Studio Settings.

Choose the correct destination

Open Settings → Domains. Artist website covers your artist pages, music, projects and gallery. Artist store is the separate shop destination. Use distinct hostnames, for example yourname.com for the website and shop.yourname.com for the store. A website request does not create a shop.

Add a domain you control

Enter only the hostname, without https://, a path, port or wildcard. Saving creates a private request; it does not purchase a domain or change DNS. Pending requests can be cancelled. You remain responsible for registration and renewal unless a separate agreement says otherwise.

Wait for verified connection instructions

Keep existing DNS records until ownership, the hosting destination and HTTPS are confirmed. Requested, ownership verified and connected are separate states. A connected status requires verified ownership and TLS readiness; it cannot be set from the artist's browser. Do not point a domain to an invented IP address or generic hosting target.
