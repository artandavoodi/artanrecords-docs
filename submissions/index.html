Send your work

A practical guide to the current enquiry process.

Use the label form

Open Submit your music on the label website. Choose Music submission, complete the required contact fields and verification, then send. The server response confirms whether the enquiry was saved.

Share listening links

Use links you have permission to share. Check that the recipient can open them. Do not send masters, identity documents, bank details or tax records through the public form.

Correct an enquiry

Contact collaborate@artanrecords.com with the enquiry reference where available. Do not repeatedly submit the same enquiry to correct a detail.
