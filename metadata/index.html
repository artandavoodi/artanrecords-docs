Artist and release names

Use the confirmed public artist name consistently. Keep legal identity separate from public credits unless its publication is intended. Confirm the release title, version and track order.

Credit the work

Provide the contributors and their roles: composition, lyrics, performance, production, mixing, mastering and artwork where applicable. Do not infer credits from an artist name.

Existing identifiers

Preserve existing ISRC and UPC identifiers when supplied. Do not invent identifiers or assume a reissue needs new ones; confirm the recording history and distributor requirements.
